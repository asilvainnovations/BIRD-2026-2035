BIRD 2026–2035 Tawi-Tawi Validation Workshop

You will score propositions that the Bangsamoro Investment and Development Roadmap has already drafted for Tawi-Tawi from secondary data. Your answers are input to the roadmap's first recalibration. They are not an endorsement.

Before you start

  • We record your sector, municipality and scores. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your organisation is optional. It is stored for the secretariat only and is never shown to the room.
  • Results are reported in aggregate. The data is held by the MTIT Bureau of Investments (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
  • You can change an answer while voting is open. Answers are saved on this phone if the signal drops and sent when it returns.
